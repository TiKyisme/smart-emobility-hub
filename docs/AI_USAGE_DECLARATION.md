# Generative AI Usage Declaration - Draft

The project team used Generative AI as a supporting tool for tasks such as organizing assignment requirements, proposing an initial repository structure, drafting implementation scaffolding, and suggesting code/documentation improvements.

AI-generated material is not treated as authoritative by itself. The team reviews, edits, and validates the material against the lecturer's assignment and course slides, and team members remain responsible for understanding and explaining the submitted design and implementation.

Before final submission, replace this draft with the exact tools, scope, and level of contribution actually used by the team.
